COMMUNITY STEM ACCESS FUND
Request for Proposals — Cycle 2026
Issued by: Lakeshore Community Foundation
Application Deadline: May 15, 2026
Award Range: $50,000 – $150,000
========================================================

OVERVIEW

The Lakeshore Community Foundation invites 501(c)(3) nonprofit organizations to
apply for funding through the Community STEM Access Fund. This fund supports
after-school and out-of-school STEM education programs serving economically
disadvantaged youth in grades 6–12.

PURPOSE

The Foundation's goal is to close the opportunity gap in STEM education by
funding organizations that provide hands-on, mentor-led programming to students
who would not otherwise have access to quality STEM experiences.

ELIGIBILITY REQUIREMENTS

Applicants MUST meet all of the following criteria to be considered:

1. NONPROFIT STATUS
   Applicants must be a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization in good
   standing with the IRS. A copy of the IRS determination letter is required.

2. GEOGRAPHIC FOCUS
   Programs must primarily serve students located in the state of Ohio.
   Priority consideration will be given to organizations serving urban or
   rural communities with a demonstrated lack of STEM resources.

3. TARGET POPULATION
   Programs must serve students in grades 6–12 from households at or below
   185% of the federal poverty level. Proof of income-based eligibility
   screening is required.

4. ANNUAL BUDGET
   Applicants must have an annual operating budget between $100,000 and
   $2,000,000. Organizations with budgets outside this range are not eligible.
   A current audited financial statement or IRS Form 990 is required.

5. PROGRAM TRACK RECORD
   Applicants must have operated their STEM program for at least two full
   program years prior to this application. Evidence of program history,
   such as annual reports or evaluation summaries, is required.

6. STAFFING
   Programs must employ or engage at least two trained STEM educators or
   mentors. Resumes or staff bios are required.

PROGRAM REQUIREMENTS

All funded programs must:

- Provide a minimum of 60 hours of STEM instruction per student per year
- Serve at least 25 students per program year
- Collect and report student attendance, participation, and learning outcomes
- Demonstrate a clear connection between program activities and STEM career
  pathways (e.g., robotics, coding, engineering challenges, science research)

PREFERRED ATTRIBUTES (not required, but strengthen the application)

- Partnership with a local school district or library
- Use of nationally recognized STEM curriculum (e.g., FIRST, Code.org, STEMfuse)
- History of placing students in STEM internships or higher education
- Matching funds or in-kind contributions from other sources
- Demonstrated community need supported by census or school data

APPLICATION REQUIREMENTS

Each application must include:

1. Completed application form (attached)
2. IRS determination letter (501(c)(3) status)
3. Most recent audited financial statements or IRS Form 990
4. Program budget for the proposed grant period
5. Narrative responses to all questions below
6. At least two letters of support from community partners
7. Student demographic data from the most recent program year

NARRATIVE QUESTIONS

Q1. Mission and Alignment
Describe your organization's mission and how your STEM program aligns with
the goals of the Community STEM Access Fund. (500 words max)

Q2. Program Description
Provide a detailed description of your program, including activities, curriculum,
mentors, schedule, and how students are recruited and retained. (800 words max)

Q3. Target Population and Community Need
Describe the community you serve and provide data demonstrating the need for
STEM programming in this community. (400 words max)

Q4. Outcomes and Evaluation
Describe how you measure the success of your program. List at least three
measurable outcomes you will track during the grant period. (400 words max)

Q5. Sustainability
Describe your organization's plan to sustain this program beyond the grant
period. Include other funding sources and earned revenue if applicable.
(300 words max)

BUDGET GUIDANCE

Funds may be used for:
- Staff salaries and benefits (up to 60% of the award)
- Curriculum materials, equipment, and supplies
- Student transportation to program site
- Evaluation and data collection costs

Funds may NOT be used for:
- Capital improvements or construction
- Indirect costs above 15% of the total award
- Out-of-state travel
- Lobbying or political activity

REPORTING REQUIREMENTS

Grantees will submit:
- A midpoint progress report at 6 months
- A final report at 12 months including participant outcomes data
- Receipts and financial documentation upon request

CONTACT

Questions should be submitted in writing to grants@lakeshorecf.org by
April 30, 2026. No phone inquiries will be accepted.
