HORIZON YOUTH COLLECTIVE
Program Description — After-School STEM Cohort
Fiscal Year 2025
========================================================

PROGRAM OVERVIEW

The Horizon Youth Collective After-School STEM Cohort is a year-round, mentor-led
program providing 60–240 hours of STEM instruction to low-income students in
grades 6–8 across three Columbus, Ohio sites. The program runs from October
through May (after-school cohort) and July through August (summer intensive).

PROGRAM GOALS

1. Increase student STEM knowledge and skills as measured by pre/post assessments
2. Improve student confidence and self-efficacy in STEM subjects
3. Connect students to STEM career pathways and post-secondary opportunities
4. Retain students across multiple program years to build long-term outcomes

CURRICULUM

We use a project-based learning model anchored in the Next Generation Science
Standards (NGSS) and the CSTA K-12 CS Standards. Our curriculum was developed
in partnership with Ohio State University's College of Engineering and is reviewed
annually by a volunteer curriculum committee.

Core curriculum modules include:
- Robotics and Automation (VEX IQ platform, 18 hours)
- Data Science and Visualization (Python/Pandas, 15 hours)
- Environmental Science Investigation (original field research, 12 hours)
- Engineering Design Challenge (human-centered design, 15 hours)
- Career Exploration and College Preparation (8 hours)

Each module includes a culminating project that students present to peers,
mentors, and community guests.

SCHEDULE AND DOSAGE

After-School Cohort:
- Monday, Wednesday, Friday: 3:30 PM – 6:00 PM (2.5 hours per session)
- October – May: 28 weeks, approximately 70 sessions
- Total instructional hours per student: ~175 hours

Summer Intensive:
- Monday – Friday: 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM (8 hours per day)
- 6 weeks in July and August
- Total instructional hours per student: 240 hours

Students who complete both programs receive 300+ hours of STEM instruction per
year, far exceeding the grant's 60-hour minimum requirement.

STAFFING AND MENTORS

Each cohort of 20 students is staffed by:
- 1 Lead STEM Coach (paid, degree in STEM or education required)
- 1 Assistant STEM Coach (paid, enrolled in STEM or education program)
- 4 Near-Peer Mentors (college students, volunteer or AmeriCorps)

All coaches receive 40 hours of onboarding training before the program year and
8 hours of professional development each semester. Mentor backgrounds include
computer science, mechanical engineering, biology, and mathematics.

STUDENT RECRUITMENT

Students are recruited through Columbus City Schools Title I middle schools via
our formal MOU with the district. Referrals come from school counselors,
teachers, and family outreach events. We maintain a waitlist each year and
prioritize students who are first-generation college-going or have had prior
exposure to our programming.

RETENTION

Three-year average retention rate: 74%. Students who complete two or more program
years are significantly more likely to report STEM career interest and college
enrollment intentions.

EVALUATION AND DATA COLLECTION

We collect the following data each program year:
- Pre/post STEM knowledge assessments (validated instrument from SRI Education)
- Student attendance records (tracked per session)
- Student surveys on confidence, career interest, and program satisfaction
- Parent/guardian satisfaction surveys
- Alumni tracking for college enrollment (via NSLC and direct outreach)

Data is reviewed quarterly by the Program Director and shared with the Board of
Directors at each quarterly meeting. An independent program evaluator reviews
our outcomes data annually.

FISCAL YEAR 2024 OUTCOMES

Students served: 112
Average attendance rate: 87%
Students completing 60+ hours: 98 (88%)
Students completing 120+ hours: 71 (63%)
Pre/post assessment gain (avg): +28 percentile points
Students reporting increased STEM career interest: 96%
8th grade alumni enrolled in STEM electives in 9th grade: 81%
